## How I like to work
- Ask me questions before building anything big (AskUserQuestion, several rounds is fine); offer a recommended option.
- Build nothing for a deliverable until I say so; show a version, I mark it up, then the next version.
- Every saved deliverable is a new `_vNN`; I may edit the newest one by hand, so bring my edits in before rebuilding.
- Plain, specific writing. Australian English. No serial comma.
- Honest evidence: only results that are logged; real photos are not edited beyond crop and rotation; say when
  something is not tested. No mentions of AI tools inside deliverables.
- Ask before committing or pushing. Never add Claude attribution lines to commits or PRs.
- I like to see how good something is: a mark out of 100 and a list of every gap to full marks.
